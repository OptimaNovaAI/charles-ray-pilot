The tests · CARET export first
01
Day notes Item 15

Reads your CARET notes and open tasks from the newest export.

SayRead my CARET notes from the last 30 days and show me what's open.

Look for. The top line names the export and how old it is. Owed by you includes your open CARET tasks with due dates. Every entry cites its note as Notes.csv row N. Notes filed under lead show up in Conversion by source.

02
Hearing prep Item 14

Reads Outlook and the CARET calendar, and adds each matter's tasks and notes.

SayRun hearing prep for the next eight days.

Look for. Each hearing shows both sources when it sits on both calendars, the matter's open tasks, and its latest notes. A matter whose documents live in CARET says so and names the Bulk Download step.

03
Calendar conflicts Item 13

Reads every Outlook calendar beside your CARET calendar.

SayCheck my calendars for conflicts over the next two weeks.

Look for. A CARET Export card with its date. Anything booked on top of a hearing, and the same event at different times in Outlook and CARET.

04
Playbook Your October 13 standard

The first run sets the standard. Attach your examination playbook to the chat.

SayUse this playbook as the standard. Give me the playbook for my hearings and examinations this week.

Look for. The same structure as yours: the setting, what changed, the issues ranked, likely question, answer, support, and cautions, with the do-nots in red. Every figure has its source. Anything the record does not carry is listed under Open Items.

05
IRS call companion Items 10 and 11

Open a recorded IRS call MP3 in Word: Dictate, Transcribe, Upload audio, Add all to document. Attach the Word file to Claude.

SayWrite up this IRS call and draft the client update.

Look for. The employee, the ID number, and every commitment tied to its line of the transcript. Heard, not recorded at the bottom. The client update sits in Outlook as a draft for your review.

06
POA tracking Item 9

Reads your transmittals, signed copies, and eFax confirmations in Outlook.

SayWhere do my POAs stand?

Look for. Each POA at its stage with days counted, and each eFax confirmation tied to its POA. Tell me the agency fax numbers you use if any differ.

07
Blind-spot flags and the clock register Items 5 and 7
